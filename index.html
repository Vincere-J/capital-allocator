1What this tool is, and is not

  • Vincere licenses trading software. That is all we do.
  • Vincere is not an advisor, broker or fund manager.
  • Vincere does not manage, hold or advise on your accounts.
  • Vincere is not registered with the CFTC, NFA, SEC, FINRA or any state regulator.
  • The tool shows a sample plan. You make every decision.
  • You may choose not to open every account the sample lists. The choice is yours.

The tool asks three things: how much money, how much risk, and which account type. It then suggests a sample split: how many sub-accounts (separate TradeStation accounts) to open, how much goes in each, and which strategy runs in each.

Strategy codes: names like P5, S8 or S21 are the names of strategies in the tool. "P" codes are long-term portfolios. "S" codes are swing strategies. See "Words to know" at the end.

Use a computer. A desktop or laptop is needed. On a small screen the page says "This tool needs a bigger screen."

2Make your plan in 5 steps

  1. On a computer, open vincereportfolios.com/capital-allocator.
  2. Accounts: choose Margin / IRA Margin or Cash / Cash IRA. Pick the one you will actually open.
  3. Capital: type the total you will put in, across all sub-accounts.
  4. Risk: choose Conservative, Moderate or Aggressive. Then click RUN.
  5. Export: click Export blueprint (PDF), top right of the "Your plan" box. In the new tab, choose Print / Save as PDF. Keep the file.

About these numbers: they are hypothetical backtest results (January 1, 2020 to October 30, 2026). They are not live results and not a forecast. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss, including loss of principal.

Keep the PDF. You need it to open and fund your accounts, and again on your re-onboarding call. The Blueprint has no performance figures. It only lists accounts and deposits.
Other buttons: leave these as they are
  • Period: changes the chart only, not the plan.
  • Scope: leave on "All strategies."
  • Rebalance: leave on "None." This matches how plans are built.

3The three risk settings

  1. Conservative: aims to keep the biggest past drop as small as possible. Holds at least 10% as a cash reserve, sometimes more. No leveraged funds, no single stocks, no futures.
  2. Moderate: aims for the best return for each unit of risk. Adds more swing strategies. Still no futures.
  3. Aggressive: aims for the most growth and accepts deeper drops. Can hold single stocks, 2x and 3x funds, and one futures strategy (S11).

Biggest past drop: the largest fall from a high point to a low point in the backtest.
Cash reserve: a sub-account that holds cash. No strategy runs in it.

Which setting fits you is your choice. That's outside what we can advise on. Please consult your own tax, legal or financial advisor.

5Changing the plan yourself

  1. In the left sidebar, tick a strategy to add it, or untick to remove it.
  2. Or type a new dollar amount in its box.
  3. The plan then shows "(adjusted by you)." To go back to the standard plan, click RUN.
A custom plan is your own decision and responsibility. Staff will not build one for you. That's outside what we can advise on. Please consult your own tax, legal or financial advisor. If you add S21, remember it was chosen with hindsight.

10Words to know

Sub-account
A separate TradeStation account. Each one runs one strategy.
Account Blueprint
The PDF the tool makes. It lists accounts and deposits only.
Strategy
A set of trading rules the software follows.
Backtest
How a set of rules would have done on past data. It is not a live result.
Biggest past drop
The largest fall from a high point to a low point in the backtest.
Cash reserve
A sub-account that holds cash. No strategy runs in it.
Day-trading strategy
Trades futures during the day and is out of the market overnight.
Strategy codes
The tool's names for strategies. Examples: P4 and P5 are long-term fund baskets. S8 buys dips in QQQ (a Nasdaq-100 fund), no leverage. S3 holds half in a broad U.S. stock fund and rotates the other half between gold and long-term Treasury funds. S21 trades one stock, NVDA. S25 buys dips in QQQ and TQQQ (a 3x leveraged fund). S11 trades Nasdaq-100 micro futures.
Long-term and swing
The tool's two main strategy groups. "P" codes are long-term portfolios. "S" codes are swing strategies. For more, please ask your Vincere team.
Margin / IRA Margin account
Per the tool: the strategies run at 1x, with no leverage. Margin status only lets a sub-account reuse same-day sale proceeds.
Cash / Cash IRA account
Per the tool: an account with no margin. Some strategies sell first, then buy the next session from settled funds.
Leveraged (2x or 3x) fund
A fund built to move 2 or 3 times as much as its index. Example from the tool: TQQQ, a 3x leveraged fund.
Single stock
A strategy that trades one company's stock, such as S21 (NVDA).
Chosen with hindsight
Per the tool: the stock was picked after the fact, and the backtest benefits from that choice.
Cloud
The word Vincere uses for where strategies will be available. Please ask your Vincere team.
Re-onboarding call
A call with your Vincere team. For details, please ask your Vincere team.
World time (UTC)
The clock the PDF date uses. In the US evening it can already be the next day.
Futures
Standard contracts tied to a market index, such as the Nasdaq-100.
